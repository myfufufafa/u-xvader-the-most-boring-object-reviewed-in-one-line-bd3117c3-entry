## The entry

`main.py` writes exactly one line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> This doorstop has held the same door open for six years. No thank-you, no raise, no day off. The most loyal employee in this house, and it isn't even paid.

## Why this line

The doorstop's dullness is the joke. It has done exactly one thing, holding the same door open, for six years — no thank-you, no raise, no day off — and the last words sharpen it: the most loyal employee in the house, and it isn't even paid. True of every doorstop.
